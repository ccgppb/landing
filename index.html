About GPPB-TSO

Supporting a fair and effective procurement system

The Government Procurement Policy Board - Technical Support Office (GPPB-TSO) provides technical and administrative support to the Government Procurement Policy Board.

Through policy guidance, information, and learning resources, GPPB-TSO supports government agencies and procurement practitioners in carrying out public procurement.
