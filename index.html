About the Administrative Division

Supporting the people and operations behind GPPB-TSO

The Administrative Division provides internal logistics, facilities management, and general administrative services to support the day-to-day operations of the Government Procurement Policy Board - Technical Support Office (GPPB-TSO).

By coordinating essential internal services, the Division helps create a well-supported workplace so GPPB-TSO can focus on its government procurement mandate.
